# Darker background revision

Tool: built-in image_gen. The user requested darker backgrounds after the typography revision. Only the surrounding backdrop was targeted; captions and headline typography were retained. Selected outputs replace `generated/` and final exports. Exports are tagged sRGB.

The supplied App Store reference is Display P3. Reference color samples were converted to sRGB before comparison. Its mid-to-lower gradient samples at 50%, 75%, and 90% are approximately `#22587B`, `#163F5C`, and `#0B3049`. The final revision asks for a visibly darker fade after the earlier backgrounds remained too bright.

## backword

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Input 1 is the marketing artwork to edit. Input 2 shows the existing App Store artwork inside a black carousel viewer.
REPLACE ONLY THE EXTERNAL BACKGROUND of input 1 with a SIGNIFICANTLY DARKER smooth blue-to-navy gradient. The current backdrop is still too luminous, particularly beside the lower half of the device. A barely perceptible adjustment is insufficient. This must be an obvious darkening of the surrounding blue, preserving the bright white headline and the exact current screen exposure.
Use a restrained dark slate-blue top around #336E96, fading early and steadily through #244F70 at 25%, #193C55 at 50%, #102B40 at 75%, to very dark blue-teal #071E2E at 90% and #061923 at bottom. These are background colors only; do not apply a global exposure filter. No luminous cyan, blue glow, spotlights, texture, haze, grain or vignette. The lower two-thirds should read as dark navy/teal rather than bright saturated blue.
The reference card's background is subdued and dark; ignore the viewer's pure black margins, close icon and outer status bar. Keep the output full-bleed rectangular.
Everything other than the surrounding backdrop must remain exactly as in input 1: current white headline "Guess forwards." / "Reveal backwards." with its SAME font, size, weight, positioning and line breaks; current black device frame and placement; full app screen with all original colors, logos, labels, letters, icons, tiles and keyboard. Do not shrink or enlarge the headline. Keep the bright whites bright. Do not darken the app content. No new text or imagery.
Output opaque PNG portrait 1242×2688.
```
